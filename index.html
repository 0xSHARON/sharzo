// 04. RAPID DEPLOYMENT

4 STEPS TO DOMINANCE
STEP 01

ENABLE USB DEBUG

Go to Android Settings > About Phone. Tap Build Number 7 times until Developer Options are unlocked. Turn ON USB Debugging.

STEP 02

PLUG IN CABLE

Connect your phone to your PC via a USB cable. On your phone screen, check "Always allow from this computer" and press OK.

.\adb.exe devices
STEP 03

IGNITE SHARZO

Double-click sharzo.exe for raw instant CLI mirroring, or open SHARZO HUD.exe for the Cyberpunk control center.

.\sharzo.exe
STEP 04

STREAM & PLAY

Your Android screen appears instantly on your Windows monitor with sub-15ms response. Add directly as an OBS capture source or game with KBM.
