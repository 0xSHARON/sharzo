04. RAPID DEPLOYMENT

4 STEPS TO DOMINANCE
01

ENABLE USB DEBUG

Go to Android Settings > About Phone. Tap Build Number 7 times to unlock Developer Options. Turn ON USB Debugging.

02

PLUG IN CABLE

Connect your phone to your PC via a USB cable. On your phone screen, check "Always allow from this computer" and press OK.

.\adb.exe devices
03

IGNITE SHARZO

Double-click sharzo.exe for raw instant CLI mirroring, or unzip SHARZO HUD.zip and launch SHARZO HUD.exe for the visual dashboard.

.\sharzo.exe
04

STREAM & PLAY

Your phone screen appears with sub-15ms response. Add directly as an OBS Game Capture source or play mobile games with mouse & keyboard.
